# Exercise Plan — DRAFT (to refine together)

Status: first draft, not yet agreed on frequency/progression. Nothing here should
cause pain — mild, brief tingling during a nerve glide is normal; sharp pain or
numbness that lingers afterward means back off.

## 1. Median nerve glides

Classic 6-position tendon/nerve glide sequence. Move slowly between positions, hold
each ~2–3 seconds, no bouncing.

1. Make a fist.
2. Open hand, fingers straight, thumb resting alongside fingers.
3. Wrist and fingers extended back, thumb still alongside fingers.
4. Same as 3, thumb extended out to the side.
5. Same as 4, forearm rotated to turn palm up.
6. Use the other hand to gently pull the thumb back a bit further.

*Proposed: 1 round of the sequence, 2–3x/day. Adjust once tried.*

## 2. Wrist mobility / stretches

- **Prayer stretch**: palms together at chest height, lower hands while keeping
  palms touching until a gentle stretch is felt in the wrists/forearms.
- **Wrist flexor stretch**: arm out, palm up, gently pull fingers back with the
  other hand.
- **Wrist extensor stretch**: arm out, palm down, gently pull fingers/hand down and
  back with the other hand.

*Proposed: hold 15–20s each, 2–3x/day, especially before/after computer sessions.*

## 3. Strengthening

Goal: balance out the flexor-dominant pattern that typing and gripping builds, plus
general hand/forearm strength.

- **Rubber band finger extensions**: band around all fingertips, open hand against
  the resistance. Directly trains the extensor side, which is usually the weak link.
- **Wrist curls / reverse wrist curls**: light dumbbell or resistance band, slow
  and controlled, full range.
- **Grip work**: soft stress ball or light grip trainer — not a max-effort grip
  strengthener while symptoms are active.
- **Farmer carries**: light weight, walk holding it, neutral wrist — good low-risk
  grip + forearm loading.

*Proposed: start light, 2–3x/week, progress load only if no next-day flare-up.*

## 4. Circulation warm-ups

- Arm swings / shaking out the hands.
- Wrist circles, both directions.
- Warm water soak (or just running warm water over hands/wrists) before exercising
  or before a heavy computer session.
- General cardio — even just consistent movement during the day — helps overall
  circulation, not just local hand work.

## 5. Push-up wrist tolerance progression

Since closed-fist push-ups are already comfortable and open-hand push-ups still
cause some wrist discomfort, the idea is a gradual bridge rather than jumping
straight back to full open-hand push-ups:

1. Push-ups on closed fists (current comfortable baseline).
2. Push-ups on parallettes / dumbbells / push-up handles — hands stay in a
   neutral (not extended) grip, but off closed fists.
3. Push-ups with fingertips only — partial extension, controlled.
4. Open-hand push-ups on an incline (less wrist extension angle than flat ground).
5. Open-hand push-ups on flat ground.

*Proposed: only move to the next step once the current one is fully comfortable for
a couple of sessions. No need to rush this.*
